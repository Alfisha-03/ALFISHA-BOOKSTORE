ALFISHA BOOKSTORE · OUR STORY
01 — Who We Are

A thoughtful approach to book management.

ALFISHA BOOKSTORE is a web-based bookstore management project created to organize book records through a clear and accessible interface.

The system connects a website with a database to maintain information such as book titles, authors, categories, prices and stock quantities.

Separate pages help users browse books, explore categories, add new records, search for information and review inventory details.

“Our purpose is simple: make book information easier to organize, find and maintain.”

Everything has its place.
